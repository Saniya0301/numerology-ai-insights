# Soul Urge Number 22 (Master Number)

## Core Meaning
Soul Urge Number 22 is a Master Number — an intensified version of the 4.
Beneath the surface, this person carries a deep inner longing to build
something of lasting, large-scale significance.

## Inner Desires
- A deep craving to create something substantial that outlives them
- Wants their practical efforts to serve a larger vision or purpose
- Motivated by both stability and meaningful impact simultaneously
- Desires to feel that their discipline and hard work truly matter

## Emotional Needs
This person needs to feel their structured, disciplined effort is
building toward something meaningful — not just busywork. Feeling like
their work lacks purpose can be deeply unsettling.

## How This Differs From Outward Behavior
Even a modest or reserved-seeming person with a 22 Soul Urge may carry
an intense inner ambition toward large-scale impact that isn't visible
in their outward humility.

## Growth Direction
Learning to pace their large ambitions realistically, and to find
satisfaction in incremental progress rather than only the final,
large-scale outcome.
