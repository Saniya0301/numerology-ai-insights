# Soul Urge Number 5

## Core Meaning
Soul Urge Number 5 reveals an inner desire for freedom and variety.
Beneath the surface, this person is deeply motivated by new experiences,
movement, and a resistance to feeling confined.

## Inner Desires
- A deep craving for freedom — physical, emotional, or circumstantial
- Wants variety and novelty rather than routine
- Motivated by adventure and the thrill of the unknown
- Desires to avoid feeling trapped or tied down

## Emotional Needs
This person needs enough flexibility in their life to feel unrestricted.
Rigid routines, excessive commitment pressure, or feeling boxed in can
create real inner restlessness, even if they don't always show it.

## How This Differs From Outward Behavior
Even a naturally steady or committed-seeming person with a 5 Soul Urge
may carry a quiet inner pull toward more freedom and variety than their
outward stability suggests.

## Growth Direction
Learning to find freedom within commitment rather than seeing them as
opposites — depth and stability don't have to mean losing independence.
