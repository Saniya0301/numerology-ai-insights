# Soul Urge Number 2

## Core Meaning
Soul Urge Number 2 reveals an inner desire for connection and harmony.
Beneath the surface, this person is deeply motivated by close
relationships, peace, and a sense of belonging with others.

## Inner Desires
- A deep craving for close, meaningful relationships
- Wants harmony and to avoid conflict wherever possible
- Motivated by being needed and appreciated by others
- Desires emotional closeness and mutual understanding

## Emotional Needs
This person needs reassurance and genuine emotional connection. Feeling
excluded, unappreciated, or caught in ongoing conflict can be deeply
unsettling, even if they don't always voice it directly.

## How This Differs From Outward Behavior
Even a naturally assertive or independent-seeming person with a 2 Soul
Urge may carry a quiet, unspoken need for closeness and validation that
isn't obvious from their outward confidence.

## Growth Direction
Learning to voice their own needs directly instead of only sensing and
accommodating others' — genuine harmony includes honest expression, not
just peacekeeping.
