# Expression Number 4

## Core Meaning
Expression Number 4 reveals a natural talent for structure, discipline, and
practical execution. This number shows itself in someone capable of taking
an idea and methodically building it into something solid and reliable.

## Natural Talents
- Strong organizational and planning ability
- Reliable follow-through; finishes what they start
- Practical problem-solving grounded in realistic assessment
- Attention to detail and systems-thinking
- Steady work ethic capable of sustained, long-term effort

## How This Shows Outwardly
People with a 4 Expression are often seen as dependable, thorough, and
trustworthy with responsibility. Others tend to rely on them to actually
get things done, not just talk about doing them.

## Best Expressed Through
Project management, engineering, operations, accounting, construction, or
any pursuit rewarding precision, order, and dependable execution.

## Watch For
This talent can turn rigid if overused — a 4 Expression benefits from
occasionally welcoming flexibility or unconventional approaches rather
than defaulting to "the proper way" every time.
