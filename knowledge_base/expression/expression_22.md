# Expression Number 22 (Master Number)

## Core Meaning
Expression Number 22 is a Master Number — the "Master Builder" — an
intensified version of the 4. It reveals a rare talent for turning
large-scale vision into concrete, lasting reality.

## Natural Talents
- Combines big-picture vision with practical, systematic execution
- Capable of managing large, complex, long-term projects
- Strong discipline paired with genuinely ambitious goals
- Skilled at building structures — organizations, systems, institutions —
  that endure
- High capacity for sustained, high-level responsibility

## How This Shows Outwardly
People with a 22 Expression are often seen as exceptionally capable,
combining visionary ideas with the practical follow-through to actually
realize them. Others may be surprised by the scale of what they can
accomplish.

## Best Expressed Through
Large-scale entrepreneurship, architecture, engineering, institutional
leadership, or any pursuit involving building something substantial and
lasting.

## Watch For
This talent can create intense self-pressure — a 22 Expression benefits
from pacing large projects realistically rather than trying to achieve
everything at once.
