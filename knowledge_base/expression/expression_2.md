# Expression Number 2

## Core Meaning
Expression Number 2 reveals a natural talent for cooperation, diplomacy,
and relational harmony. This number shows itself in someone gifted at
reading people, mediating tension, and building genuine partnerships.

## Natural Talents
- Skilled mediator, able to find common ground between opposing views
- High emotional attunement; picks up on unspoken dynamics easily
- Natural collaborator, works well within teams and partnerships
- Patient and detail-oriented, good at supportive or coordinating roles
- Genuine listening ability that makes others feel heard

## How This Shows Outwardly
People with a 2 Expression are often seen as approachable, fair, and easy
to work with. Others tend to trust them as a sounding board or mediator,
even in tense situations.

## Best Expressed Through
Counseling, partnership-based work, coordination or diplomacy roles, and
any pursuit where relationship-building and cooperation are central to
success.

## Watch For
This talent can be underused if a 2 Expression stays too far in the
background — actively contributing their perspective (not just supporting
others') fully realizes the gift.
