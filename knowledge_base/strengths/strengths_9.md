# Core Strengths: Life Path 9

## Primary Strengths
Life Path 9's defining strength is genuine perspective-taking — the
ability to see a situation from multiple angles simultaneously,
including angles that directly affect people other than themselves.
This shows up as wisdom that goes beyond pure self-interest.

## How These Strengths Show Up Day-to-Day
In practical terms, this number is often the one who points out an
overlooked consequence of a decision, or who advocates for a
perspective that wouldn't otherwise be represented in the room. Their
compassion tends to be genuinely broad, extending well beyond their
immediate circle.

## Underlying Source
This strength stems from a genuine capacity for empathy that extends
outward — Life Path 9 tends to naturally imagine how a situation feels
from someone else's position, not as an effort but as a default way of
processing information.

## Where This Strength Adds the Most Value
This strength is most valuable in situations requiring genuine
big-picture, values-driven judgment — humanitarian work, ethical
decision-making, or any context where considering broader impact
matters as much as immediate practical outcomes.
