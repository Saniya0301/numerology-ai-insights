# Life Path Number 22 (Master Number)

## Core Meaning
Life Path 22 is a Master Number — often called the "Master Builder." It's
an intensified version of the 4, combining the visionary insight of 11
with the practical, structural discipline of 4. 22s are here to turn big
ideas into large-scale, lasting reality.

## Strengths
- Rare combination of visionary thinking and practical execution
- Capable of large-scale planning and long-term project management
- Strong discipline paired with genuinely ambitious goals
- Natural ability to turn abstract ideas into concrete systems or institutions
- High potential for significant, lasting achievement or impact

## Challenges
- The gap between their big vision and current reality can create intense
  self-pressure
- Risk of burnout from carrying outsized ambition and responsibility
- Can become overly controlling when trying to manage large, complex plans
- May doubt their own capability despite strong evidence of competence
- Difficulty relaxing; can feel guilty when not being productive

## Career Tendencies
22s often thrive in large-scale entrepreneurship, architecture, engineering,
institutional leadership, urban planning, or any field involving building
something substantial and lasting. They tend to feel unfulfilled in
small-scale or purely routine work that doesn't use their full capacity.

## Relationship Tendencies
22s bring stability and depth to relationships, though their intense focus
on goals can sometimes overshadow personal connection. Their growth edge is
including loved ones in their vision rather than carrying it entirely alone.

## Growth Direction
The developmental path for a 22 is pacing themselves — building
sustainably rather than trying to achieve everything at once, and allowing
support from others rather than shouldering it all solo.
