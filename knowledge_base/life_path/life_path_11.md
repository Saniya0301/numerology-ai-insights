# Life Path Number 11 (Master Number)

## Core Meaning
Life Path 11 is a Master Number — an intensified, higher-octave version of
the 2. It represents intuition, spiritual insight, and inspiration. 11s
often feel a strong pull toward something larger than themselves, carrying
heightened sensitivity and a visionary quality that sets them apart.

## Strengths
- Exceptionally strong intuition, often bordering on precognitive insight
- Inspirational presence; naturally uplifts and influences others
- Deep sensitivity to energy, emotion, and unspoken dynamics
- Visionary thinking, able to perceive possibilities others miss
- Often gifted in creative, spiritual, or healing-oriented pursuits

## Challenges
- Heightened sensitivity can tip into anxiety, overwhelm, or nervous energy
- Living up to the intensity of an 11 can create pressure and self-doubt
- May swing between deep confidence and deep insecurity
- Can struggle to stay grounded in practical, everyday matters
- Prone to burnout from absorbing too much of others' emotional energy

## Career Tendencies
11s often thrive in counseling, spiritual or healing work, the arts,
teaching, and any field requiring inspiration, vision, or intuitive
insight. Grounding practices and structure (borrowing from the base
number 2) help them channel their gifts sustainably.

## Relationship Tendencies
11s form deep, intuitive emotional bonds and often sense their partner's
needs before they're spoken. Their growth edge is managing their own
sensitivity so they don't lose themselves in a partner's emotional world.

## Growth Direction
The developmental path for an 11 is learning to ground visionary insight
into practical action — translating inspiration into something tangible,
rather than staying purely in the intuitive or emotional realm.
