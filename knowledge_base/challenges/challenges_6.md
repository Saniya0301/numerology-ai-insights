# Growth Challenges: Life Path 6

## Primary Challenge Pattern
Life Path 6's defining growth edge is overextension — taking on more
responsibility for others' wellbeing than is genuinely sustainable,
often at real cost to their own needs and energy.

## How This Shows Up Day-to-Day
In practical terms, this number can say yes to caretaking requests they
don't have capacity for, or feel guilty setting a boundary even when
one is clearly needed. Over time, this can lead to quiet burnout that
isn't always visible to the people they're supporting.

## Underlying Root
This pattern often stems from a genuine belief that their worth is tied
to how much they give — pulling back on caretaking can feel, to this
number, like a form of failure or selfishness rather than healthy
self-preservation.

## Constructive Growth Path
Growth here involves practicing boundary-setting as an act of genuine
care (for themselves and, ultimately, for their sustainability to help
others), and consciously distributing caretaking responsibility rather
than defaulting to carrying it alone.
