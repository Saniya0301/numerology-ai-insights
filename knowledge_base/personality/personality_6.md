# Personality Number 6

## Core Meaning
Personality Number 6 reflects how a person comes across to others at
first impression. A 6 Personality projects warmth, responsibility, and
approachability as a caretaker figure.

## First Impression
- Comes across as caring, warm, and family-oriented
- Perceived as trustworthy and safe to confide in
- Seen as responsible and dependable in social settings
- Others may sense a nurturing, protective quality right away

## How Others Perceive Them
People often assume this person will look out for others' wellbeing
and may naturally turn to them for advice or comfort, even in early
interactions.

## Possible Gap With Inner Self
The outwardly nurturing, composed impression of a 6 Personality doesn't
always reflect the person's own unmet needs — someone can appear
endlessly giving while internally feeling depleted or unsupported.
Worth checking against their Soul Urge number for the fuller picture.

## Social Dynamics
Others may default to relying on this person for emotional or
practical support without checking whether they have the capacity to
give it in that moment.
