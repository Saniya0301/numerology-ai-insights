# Personality Number 2

## Core Meaning
Personality Number 2 reflects how a person comes across to others at
first impression. A 2 Personality projects warmth, approachability, and
gentleness.

## First Impression
- Comes across as kind, easy to talk to, and non-threatening
- Perceived as a good listener, even before much has been said
- Seen as cooperative and accommodating in group settings
- Others may sense genuine warmth or emotional sensitivity right away

## How Others Perceive Them
People often feel comfortable opening up to this person quickly,
sensing they won't be judged harshly. They may be seen as a natural
mediator or peacekeeper in social situations.

## Possible Gap With Inner Self
The gentle, accommodating outward impression of a 2 Personality doesn't
always reflect the person's inner opinions or needs — they may hold
strong views internally while presenting a soft, agreeable exterior.
Worth checking against their Soul Urge number for the fuller picture.

## Social Dynamics
Others may unconsciously lean on this person for emotional support or
conflict mediation, sometimes without checking whether that's actually
welcome in the moment.
