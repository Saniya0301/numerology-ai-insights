# Personality Number 22 (Master Number)

## Core Meaning
Personality Number 22 is a Master Number — an intensified version of the
4. It reflects how a person comes across to others at first impression,
projecting an unusually capable, grounded, and quietly powerful
presence.

## First Impression
- Comes across as exceptionally competent and composed
- Perceived as someone with real substance, even briefly
- Seen as steady, serious, and quietly impressive
- Others may sense significant untapped capability

## How Others Perceive Them
People often assume this person is capable of handling major
responsibility and may be drawn to entrust them with significant tasks
early in a relationship, sensing their reliability and strength.

## Possible Gap With Inner Self
The outwardly composed, capable impression of a 22 Personality doesn't
always reflect the internal pressure the person feels — someone can
appear steady while quietly carrying immense self-imposed expectation.
Worth checking against their Soul Urge number for the fuller picture.

## Social Dynamics
Others may place unusually high expectations on this person early on,
sensing their capability, which can create pressure to always perform
at a high level.
