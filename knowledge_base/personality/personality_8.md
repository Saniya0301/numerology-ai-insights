# Personality Number 8

## Core Meaning
Personality Number 8 reflects how a person comes across to others at
first impression. An 8 Personality projects authority, competence, and
strength.

## First Impression
- Comes across as capable, ambitious, and in control
- Perceived as successful or high-achieving, even at first glance
- Seen as commanding in presence, sometimes intimidating
- Others may sense strong drive or seriousness of purpose

## How Others Perceive Them
People often defer to this person's judgment in business or practical
matters and may assume they hold real authority or expertise, even
before it's demonstrated.

## Possible Gap With Inner Self
The outwardly powerful, composed impression of an 8 Personality doesn't
always reflect the person's inner vulnerability — someone can appear
unshakeable while carrying real self-doubt internally. Worth checking
against their Soul Urge number for the fuller picture.

## Social Dynamics
Others may hesitate to offer this person emotional support, assuming
they don't need it based on their outward strength — which can leave
genuine vulnerability unaddressed.
