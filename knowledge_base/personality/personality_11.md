# Personality Number 11 (Master Number)

## Core Meaning
Personality Number 11 is a Master Number — an intensified version of the
2. It reflects how a person comes across to others at first impression,
projecting an unusually intuitive, magnetic, or striking presence.

## First Impression
- Comes across as intense, perceptive, or unusually magnetic
- Perceived as having a distinctive, memorable presence
- Seen as intuitive or emotionally attuned very quickly by others
- Others may sense a spiritual or inspiring quality right away

## How Others Perceive Them
People often feel drawn to this person or notice something striking
about their energy, even without being able to fully articulate why.
They may be seen as inspiring or unusually insightful.

## Possible Gap With Inner Self
The outwardly composed or magnetic impression of an 11 Personality can
mask genuine inner overwhelm — someone can appear calm and captivating
while carrying intense internal sensitivity. Worth checking against
their Soul Urge number for the fuller picture.

## Social Dynamics
Others may project expectations of profound insight or inspiration onto
this person, which can create pressure to always seem wise or
"switched on."
