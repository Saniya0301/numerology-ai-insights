# Compatibility: Old Soul Group (Numbers 9, 11, 22, 33)

## Old Soul + Old Soul
Often considered one of the most naturally compatible pairings — both
partners share a deep sense of purpose, compassion, and broader
perspective on life. This pairing tends to feel like a meeting of
genuinely kindred spirits. The shared risk is both partners setting
unrealistically high ideals for the relationship, or both over-giving
to causes and other people at the expense of the relationship itself.

## General Old Soul Compatibility Notes
Old Soul numbers (9, 11, 22, 33) tend to seek relationships with real
depth and meaning rather than surface connection. They generally pair
well with partners who share or at least respect their broader,
service-oriented worldview. Relationships that stay purely practical or
transactional tend to leave an Old Soul feeling unfulfilled over time.
