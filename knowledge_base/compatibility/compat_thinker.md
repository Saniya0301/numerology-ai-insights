# Compatibility: Thinker Group (Numbers 4, 7)

## Thinker + Thinker
A stable, deeply understanding pairing. Both partners value depth,
reflection, and structure, and tend to respect each other's need for
solitude and independent processing. The risk is the relationship
becoming too insular or routine-bound — occasionally inviting more
spontaneity keeps the connection from feeling stagnant.

## Thinker + Old Soul
A thoughtful, meaningful pairing. Both partners tend to value depth
over surface interaction, and the Old Soul's broader compassion pairs
well with the Thinker's grounded, analytical nature. Together they can
build a relationship based on genuine intellectual and emotional
substance.
