# Compatibility: Leader Group (Numbers 1, 8)

## Leader + Leader
Two Leaders together create a dynamic, ambitious pairing, but also real
potential for a power struggle. Both partners are used to taking charge,
which can create friction over decision-making and control. When they
learn to divide leadership rather than compete for it — one leading in
certain areas, the other in different ones — this pairing becomes a
powerful, mutually respecting partnership.

## Leader + Diplomat
This pairing tends to work well. The Leader's drive and directness is
balanced by the Diplomat's emotional attunement and desire for harmony.
The Diplomat helps soften the Leader's intensity, while the Leader gives
the Diplomat a sense of direction and protection. The main risk is the
Diplomat over-accommodating the Leader's dominant energy instead of
voicing their own needs.

## Leader + Creative
An energetic, exciting combination. The Leader's ambition pairs well
with the Creative's expressive, joyful energy — together they can build
something original and dynamic. Friction can arise if the Leader wants
more structure and follow-through than the Creative naturally provides.

## Leader + Thinker
This pairing requires patience. The Leader's fast, action-oriented
approach can clash with the Thinker's need for deep reflection before
acting. When both partners respect the other's pace — the Leader
allowing space for analysis, the Thinker allowing room for decisive
action — the combination can be quite complementary.

## Leader + Old Soul
A generally harmonious pairing. The Old Soul's wisdom and broader
perspective can guide the Leader's ambition toward more meaningful
purpose, while the Leader gives the Old Soul's ideals a practical push
toward action.
